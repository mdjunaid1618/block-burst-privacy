Privacy Policy for Block Burst

Effective Date: September 26, 2026 | Last Updated: September 26, 2026

Block Burst ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to safeguarding player privacy. This policy outlines how information is handled when using the Block Burst Android application ("App").

1. Information We Do NOT Collect Directly

Block Burst is a casual single-player, offline-capable puzzle game. We do not operate user accounts or require registration, and the App does not directly collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contacts.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores game progress locally on your device via HTML5 LocalStorage:

  • Classic Mode High Score
  • Daily Challenge completion records, target scores, and consecutive win streaks
  • Player audio preferences (sound effects, music, and haptics)

This game-progress and preference data is stored locally by the App. You can clear local App data through Android App Settings. Clearing local App data does not delete information that may be processed independently by third-party advertising providers.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob & UMP)

Block Burst displays optional rewarded video advertisements via Google AdMob (to earn a continue revive in Daily Challenge mode). The Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK may process:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID).
  • Device and network information: Information that may be processed by Google Mobile Ads for advertising, security, fraud prevention, measurement, and diagnostics, subject to applicable consent and configuration.
  • Advertising interactions: Information associated with ad requests, impressions, interactions, and diagnostics as described in Google's current Mobile Ads documentation.

For more details about Google’s handling of information through its advertising services, see the Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads Data Safety guidance.

4. User Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

Users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland are prompted with a consent dialog powered by Google UMP on initial launch. You can configure consent for personalized or non-personalized ads, or adjust settings in your device's Settings > Google > Ads.

5. Children's Privacy

Block Burst is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information. Advertising and consent behavior is configured according to applicable Google policies and consent requirements.
